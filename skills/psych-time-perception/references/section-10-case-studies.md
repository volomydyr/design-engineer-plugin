## How to use

When running `psych-full-scan` or this skill standalone, the case studies serve two purposes:

1. **Anchor the principle**: a concrete shipped example makes the principle stick where the abstract definition slips.
2. **Calibrate severity**: comparing the user's product to a known case study (rather than a hypothetical) makes the recommendation specific instead of generic.

## Format per case study

Each case study should follow this shape:

```
### [Principle name] ([Law N]) – [Product name]

**The setup**: One sentence on what the product does and the user moment in question.
**The principle in action**: How the product's design embodies (or violates) the principle.
**The outcome**: Observable result – conversion lift, retention shift, complaint pattern, etc. Cite if the source is public.
**Why it matters here**: Why this case study is the canonical example for this principle (vs. other examples).
```
