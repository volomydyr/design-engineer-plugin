# Transcript: Full Research Pipeline (Without Skill)

## Context
- **User request**: Run the full research pipeline for a new pet sitting marketplace idea, starting from zero.
- **Skill loaded**: None. This is a baseline test of what Claude Code does without the design-engineer skill.

---

## Steps Taken

### Step 1: Acknowledged the Idea
Recognized this as an early-stage product idea. Offered to help think through the concept but did not follow any structured pipeline or load any skills.

### Step 2: Brainstormed the Concept
Provided general thoughts on a pet sitting marketplace:
- Market opportunity: Growing pet ownership, post-COVID travel recovery
- Business model: Commission-based marketplace (take percentage of each booking)
- Key challenges: Trust/safety, supply-demand balance, local network effects
- Comparable companies: Rover, Wag!, Care.com

### Step 3: Suggested Areas to Research
Recommended the user look into:
- Market size and growth trends for pet care services
- Existing competitors and what they do well/poorly
- Target audience segments (pet owners, pet sitters, both sides)
- Regulatory considerations (insurance, liability)
- Revenue model options (commission, subscription, freemium)

### Step 4: Offered to Help Further
Suggested specific next steps:
- "Want me to help you write a business plan outline?"
- "I can help you think through your target audience in more detail"
- "Would a competitive analysis of Rover and Wag! be useful?"

---

## What Was NOT Done (Limitations Without Skill)

1. **No pipeline concept**: Did not recognize "full" as a trigger for a structured multi-step research sequence. Treated it as a single conversation turn.
2. **No skill sequencing**: Did not work through big-idea, problem-statement, target-audience, assumptions, competitor-analysis, and storybrand in order.
3. **No structured frameworks**: Each area was covered at surface level without the depth that dedicated skills provide (no persona development, no assumptions prioritization matrix, no StoryBrand framework).
4. **No deliverable files**: All output was conversational text. No files saved to `docs/design/`.
5. **No compound documentation**: No meta-compound step to consolidate findings.
6. **No context accumulation**: Without separate skills building on each other, insights from one area don't systematically inform the next.
7. **No dependency tracking**: No `.dependencies.yaml` awareness.
8. **No optional step handling**: User interviews were mentioned in passing but not presented as a pipeline decision point.
