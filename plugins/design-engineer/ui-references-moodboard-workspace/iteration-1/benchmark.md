## Key Findings

### What the skill adds (that baseline cannot do):
1. **Categorical organization** — Groups references by domain (pet apps, marketplaces, booking) with cross-pollination
2. **Pattern extraction** — Identifies specific visual patterns across references (card layouts, color palettes, typography)
3. **Aesthetic filtering** — Positions references on aesthetic spectrums to match desired tone
4. **Approach comparison** — Structured comparison of visual approaches with pros/cons
5. **Visual vocabulary deliverable** — Produces actionable direction document to carry into Figma

### What baseline does instead:
- Eval 1: Names Dribbble and a couple competitors, gives generic "use warm colors" advice without structured collection
- Eval 2: Mentions relevant products but no systematic filtering, pattern analysis, or comparison

### Analyst Notes:
- Baseline scores low (20%) because reference gathering is a structured activity that requires methodology, not just knowledge
- Both evals show identical baseline pattern: can name sources but cannot organize, analyze, or synthesize them
- The skill's value is entirely in the framework — turning scattered suggestions into a usable visual direction
