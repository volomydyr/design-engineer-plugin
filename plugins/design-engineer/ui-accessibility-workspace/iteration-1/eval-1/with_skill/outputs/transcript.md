# Eval 94 — With Skill Transcript

**Prompt:** We need to make our healthcare patient portal accessible. We have elderly patients who may have low vision, and some patients who use screen readers. What should we check?

**Step 1:** Skill activates WCAG compliance audit framework. Identifies healthcare portal as high-priority accessibility context (WCAG AAA recommended).

**Step 2:** Runs structured audit checklist — starts with perceivable criteria: contrast ratios (recommends 7:1 AAA for primary text given elderly users), text resizing up to 200%, non-text content alternatives.

**Step 3:** Evaluates operable criteria: keyboard-only navigation paths through appointment booking, medication management, and messaging flows. Flags need for 48px minimum tap targets for elderly motor control.

**Step 4:** Assesses understandable criteria: medical terminology readability level, error messaging clarity, consistent navigation patterns across portal sections.

**Step 5:** Reviews robust criteria: screen reader compatibility with ARIA landmarks, live regions for appointment alerts, form label associations for all input fields.

**Step 6:** Adds healthcare-specific layer: emergency state accessibility, medication dosage display at high contrast, appointment confirmation multi-modal feedback (visual + audio).

**Step 7:** Produces prioritized accessibility audit report — 8 P0 blockers (missing form labels, no skip nav, contrast failures), 12 P1 critical items, 15 P2 improvements.

**Step 8:** Generates remediation roadmap with estimated effort per item and suggested sprint allocation.

**Result:** Comprehensive WCAG audit with healthcare-specific overlay, prioritized remediation plan, and elderly user accommodations.
