## Risk assessment

- **Risk**: pinning to `claude-opus-4-7` means when Anthropic ships Opus 4.8, skills won't auto-upgrade. **Mitigation**: documented trade-off; future bulk refresh planned. Beta phase.
- **Risk**: sed catches `model: opus` in unintended contexts (e.g., a docs example). **Mitigation**: anchored regex (`^...$`) only matches lines where `model: opus` is the entire line — frontmatter only. Post-sed grep audit confirms.
- **Risk**: someone running on a non-Anthropic provider (Bedrock/Vertex/Foundry) where Opus 4.7 isn't available yet. **Mitigation**: per Anthropic docs, those providers fall back to a previous version with a notice; not fatal. Documented in CHANGELOG.
- **Risk**: `xhigh` runs as `high` on Opus 4.6 per fallback rule. Users on 4.6 (e.g., Bedrock today) will see effective effort drop one level on the 5 changed skills. **Mitigation**: this is the desired behavior; xhigh is the right top-tier on 4.7, and high-on-4.6 is still high. The previous `max` had its own session-only caveat.
- **Risk**: SKILL.md docstrings or skill prose mentions "opus" or "max effort" outside frontmatter — unaffected by anchored sed. **Mitigation**: anchored regex only touches frontmatter lines, leaving prose alone. CLAUDE.md doc update covers any rule-text references.
